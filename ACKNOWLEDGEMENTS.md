## Assets

The game's placeholder models are used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), which requires that their authors be credited:

- The player and the robots are based on ["Bob The Robot"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bob-the-robot-94261177811745deb1c5a11694b5faf3), by [manoeldarochadeoliveira](https://sketchfab.com/manoeldarochadeoliveira). It is scaled to fit, and the robots' body has another colour.
- The blocks are based on ["Portal Cube"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/portal-cube-b67becc862054d9fab781b3e4030a1af), by [bobbyrazo](https://sketchfab.com/bobbyrazo). It is scaled to fit.

The dialogue's font is [Fusion Pixel](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font), by TakWolf, under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/). It includes glyphs from [Ark Pixel](https://github.com/TakWolf/ark-pixel-font), by TakWolf, [BoutiqueBitmap9x9](https://github.com/scott0107000/BoutiqueBitmap9x9), by fancy type foundry, and [Galmuri](https://github.com/quiple/galmuri), by Lee Minseo, under the same licence. The licences are beside the font, in `game/assets/fonts/fusion_pixel/`.

These placeholder sounds, from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/), are in the public domain under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), which doesn't ask for credit, but we thank their authors anyway:

- The jump: ["jumping"](https://freesound.org/s/146726/), by Leszek_Szary.
- Aiming a phase: ["teleport"](https://freesound.org/s/172207/), by Leszek_Szary.
- Throwing a phase: ["teleport"](https://freesound.org/s/172206/), by Leszek_Szary.
- Cancelling a phase or a spawn: ["Critical Error"](https://freesound.org/s/844890/), by DAN2008.
- The robots' laser: ["Electrical Shock (Zap).wav"](https://freesound.org/s/160421/), by BigKahuna360, cut to 1.5 seconds.

The door's opening and closing sounds are one of the many explosion sounds by [Iwan "qubodup" Gabovitch](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/), heavily bitcrushed. We have lost track of which one.

The music is an arrangement of Johann Sebastian Bach's Invention No. 13 in A minor, BWV 784.
